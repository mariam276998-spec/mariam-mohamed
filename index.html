Who I Am

About Me

My name is Mariam Mohamed, and I am passionate about technology, creativity, and web development. I enjoy discovering how websites work and learning how to build digital experiences that combine attractive design with clear and organized content. I believe that every new challenge is an opportunity to develop my abilities, gain experience, and improve the way I approach problems.

My interest in web development has encouraged me to learn technologies such as HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap. HTML allows me to structure web pages and organize their content, while CSS helps me create attractive layouts through colors, typography, spacing, and visual effects. Bootstrap introduces me to responsive design and reusable components that make websites easier to organize across different screen sizes.

I enjoy working on website projects because they give me the opportunity to apply what I learn in a practical way. I pay attention to the organization of page sections, the consistency of colors, the readability of text, and the overall user experience. I understand that building a good website is not only about its appearance, but also about making its content clear, its navigation simple, and its layout suitable for different devices.

I believe that continuous learning, patience, and practice are important parts of becoming a successful developer. When I encounter a problem, I try to understand its cause and find a suitable solution instead of giving up. Every project helps me identify what I can improve and encourages me to explore better ways to write and organize my code.

My ambition is to continue developing my skills, gain more practical experience, and build a strong foundation in web development. I hope to create websites that reflect creativity, attention to detail, and a commitment to quality. I am excited to keep learning, work on new ideas, and grow step by step into a more confident and capable web developer.
